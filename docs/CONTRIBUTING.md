# Review checklist

Use this checklist to guide judgement. Completion is not a substitute for understanding the mathematics.

## Problem and claim

- Is the exact statement identifiable and stable?
- Are domains, quantifiers, boundary cases and assumptions explicit?
- Does the result answer the stated question?
- Is the contribution distinguished from previously known work?

## Evidence

- Can another contributor reproduce the result from the committed inputs?
- Are numeric calculations exact where the claim requires exactness?
- If approximation is used, are error bounds and numerical limitations clear?
- Are proof dependencies and any additional assumptions visible?
- Has an independent reviewer checked that a formal statement expresses the intended informal statement?
- Is a computational search's coverage sufficient for the conclusion drawn?

## Research record

- Does the record identify the source version, toolchain, commands and outputs?
- Are rejected answers, failed checks and unresolved objections preserved?
- Is any new record linked to the earlier record it corrects?
- Are scripted demonstrations, live model attempts and independent replications labelled accurately?

## Knowledge transfer

- Does the explanation show why the method works and when it applies?
- Are at least one worked example and relevant limitation available?
- Is the transfer exercise meaningfully different from the worked example?
- Are learning claims supported by actual assessment evidence?

## Publication

- Do verification, novelty and publication status remain separate?
- Are contributor credit, licences and assistance declarations complete?
- Has any intended journal submission followed the journal's editorial process?
- Does the proposed public material exclude confidential or unauthorised content?
