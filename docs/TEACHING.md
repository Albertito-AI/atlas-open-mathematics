# Teaching and knowledge transfer

The project's educational aim is that another person can understand a method, assess its limitations and use it on an unfamiliar problem. Publishing more text does not by itself demonstrate that this has happened.

## A teaching companion

Each substantial result should have a companion appropriate to its audience. Include:

1. **The question.** Explain what is being asked and why it matters, with the necessary definitions.
2. **A worked example.** Show enough mathematical steps for a reader to reconstruct the result. Identify where a theorem or external source is used.
3. **The central method.** Explain why it works and the assumptions on which it depends.
4. **An instructive difficulty.** Show a failed approach, boundary case or tempting overgeneralisation where useful.
5. **Verification.** Provide reproduction instructions or a check that the reader can perform.
6. **Transfer.** Include a new problem that requires the same idea in a meaningfully different setting, with an answer or assessment guide separately identified.
7. **Further inquiry.** State what remains open and provide accurate references for continued study.

The initial exact-arithmetic pilot is suitable for an introductory companion about solving simultaneous equations, checking answers and recognising singular systems. Describe it as established mathematics. Any claim that its lesson improves learners' performance needs a separate evaluation.

## Levels of explanation

| Reader | Useful material |
| --- | --- |
| Curious general reader | The question, an accessible example, the reasoning and its significance. |
| Student or teacher | Prerequisites, a worked derivation, exercises, common errors and assessment guidance. |
| Research contributor | Exact statements, complete proofs or certificates, source versions, dependencies and reproducible code. |
| Agent developer | Structured task records, accepted procedures, failure cases and evaluation boundaries. |

The accounts should agree about assumptions and results. A simpler explanation must not strengthen the underlying claim or hide uncertainty.

## Measuring transfer

Before an evaluation, record the research question, comparison, scoring rules, tasks, sample and stopping conditions. Compare performance with and without the teaching material while keeping relevant conditions as similar as practicable. Report all attempted tasks and the limitations of the comparison.

For agents, useful measures include correct solutions under a fixed resource budget, repeated error rates, unsupported claims and successful application to different problem structures. Repeated trials and independent tasks are needed before attributing improvement to memory or instruction.

For people, useful measures include explanation quality, performance on unfamiliar problems, retention after an interval and ability to identify when a method does not apply. Any study involving learners needs appropriate consent and institutional review where applicable. Do not place identifiable learner records in this public repository.

The first public demo supplies a reproducible illustration, not evidence from a controlled human or live-agent learning study.

## Accessibility and reuse

Provide readable text alongside diagrams. Give equations enough verbal context for readers using assistive technology. Prefer downloadable, lightweight materials that can be used without a paid service or an account. Identify prerequisite knowledge and provide routes to it.

Translations should preserve the original statement and uncertainty labels and identify their source version. Have a competent reviewer check mathematical terminology where possible. State whether a translation has received that review.

Original companions use the repository's [CC BY 4.0 content licence](../LICENSE-CONTENT.md), except where otherwise stated. Credit contributors and preserve third-party notices. A journal article's separate licence does not automatically authorise derivative teaching material; prepare a separately authorised companion when necessary.
