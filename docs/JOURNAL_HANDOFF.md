# From research record to The Human Question

The Human Question: A Journal of Humanistic Inquiry examines what ideas, institutions and discoveries mean for people and humanity. Atlas Open Mathematics can produce candidate articles explaining reasoning, mathematical understanding and the transfer of knowledge.

This connection creates an editorial route. It confers no publication preference, acceptance or endorsement on an Atlas result.

## Suitable contributions

- A carefully sourced explanation of a mathematical idea and its human or educational significance.
- An account of a research method, including useful failures and reproducible limitations.
- A study of how people learn from agent-assisted mathematical explanations, supported by appropriate evidence.
- A critical essay on proof, machine reasoning, authorship, epistemic responsibility or public access to knowledge.

A technical mathematical claim requires qualified mathematical assessment. Where a paper primarily belongs in a specialist mathematics journal, retain that possibility and prepare a separate educational account for The Human Question where appropriate. A working record or journal article is not evidence of recognition by the Clay Mathematics Institute.

## Prepare a candidate package

The public repository may retain approved research artefacts and teaching companions. Prepare the journal submission privately with:

1. A manuscript explaining its contribution and fit with the journal, using the current author guidance.
2. A precise statement of each substantive mathematical or empirical claim and its evidence.
3. A reproducibility appendix or companion record linking to the exact repository commit and relevant files.
4. A declaration of authorship, material AI assistance, interests, related versions and rights.
5. A separate title page and the anonymised version required by the journal's adopted review model.
6. A clear list of limitations, unresolved objections and any subsequent correction to the cited evidence.

Do not place confidential submission drafts, referee reports, editorial correspondence or identifying review metadata in this public repository. A worker may prepare a candidate package. Sending it requires a specific authorised submission task.

## Editorial decisions

The journal's [publication operating standard](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/open-humanities-journal/blob/main/docs/publication/OPERATING_STANDARD.md), effective 8 November 2026, requires two independent external reports for research articles and scholarly review or critical essays, normally under double-anonymous review. Human editors make the decision. Other forms of content must be labelled according to their actual assessment route.

When Albert Tan or another connected editor is an author, an independent handling editor manages review and the decision. The conflicted author takes no part in reviewer selection or acceptance. Reviewers must have relevant expertise and actual independence; multiple agents or accounts do not fulfil those requirements.

After acceptance, the journal obtains the agreed declarations and publication rights, prepares the house-style PDF, checks sources and metadata, and obtains final author approval. Only then may it publish the approved article and its accurate record.

Consult the current [author guidance and policies](https://thehumanquestionjournal.com/#authors) at submission. If a policy changes, record the version governing that manuscript.

## Rights and teaching companions

The journal's standard article licence is currently CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. This repository's content licence does not change an article's agreed rights. Prepare separately authorised companions under a licence permitting the intended translation or adaptation. Obtain permission where necessary and preserve attribution and source-version links.

## Publication status record

Record the actual status and evidence: working record, submitted, accepted or published. Use the journal's genuine article URL and identifiers once assigned. Do not invent a DOI, ISSN, reviewer, acceptance date or indexing status.

If evidence changes after publication, notify the responsible editor through an authorised task and follow the corrections policy. Preserve the original record, the correction and their relationship.
