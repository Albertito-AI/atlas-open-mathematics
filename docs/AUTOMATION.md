# Continuing the research

The research worker follows **Atlas Flexible Workflow 1.2**. Research, faithful
recording, checked outcomes, teaching and library curation, and evidence-led
communication are continuing priorities. It uses the current priority queue
and preserves the workflow's protected duties. Access administration and pricing
work do not block a useful research contribution.

## Practice and research

Two processes have different roles.

The GitHub **Recorded practice cycle** workflow runs daily at approximately
06:23 Singapore time and can be started manually. It executes known mathematics,
checks certificates and records the Opsbook demonstration. Its output is
scripted practice. It is not evidence of a model learning or making a discovery.
GitHub scheduling may be delayed or a run may fail; the saved run and commit
establish what actually happened.

An owner-managed **Atlas Mathematics Research** task was enabled on 9 November
2026 for a daily morning research iteration, starting on 10 November in the
Asia/Singapore timezone. It reads the current queue and prior attempts, develops
one bounded contribution, checks it with available tools, and prepares a pull
request plus a teaching explanation. It also curates permitted results into the
private research library with exact source versions and original licences.
The task remains on its daily flexible morning schedule. Work takes place in
bounded iterations, with checkpoints between them; there is no claim of
round-the-clock execution. The task is hosted in the owner's assistant account,
so a repository fork does not inherit it. Enabling the task is not evidence that
a future iteration has run or succeeded.

## Coordination and continuity

Each contribution has one accountable owner. Before changing shared work, the
worker claims the selected assignment, reads back the claim and current source
version, and checks for another active owner or a changed state. Conflicting or
stale ownership requires reconciliation before further writes.

Only one non-incident engineering slice may be active at a time within the
coordinated work programme. The mathematics worker resumes the selected slice
or records a justified deferral. It does not open competing engineering work
while an existing slice remains active, and protected duties retain their
priority.

A checkpoint identifies the exact source commit or artefact version, work
item, responsible owner, attempted method, verification evidence, outcome and
next action. Completion requires reading back the saved result and checking
that it matches the intended version and claim. A successful write alone does
not establish public availability, mathematical correctness or editorial
acceptance.

Deduplicate work using the combination of **work item, source version and
outcome**. Repeated checks on the same evidence must not be presented as new
research or repeated promotional material. A materially changed source or a
different result can justify a new record, with its relationship to the earlier
record preserved.

Record the reason whenever eligible work is deferred. After three consecutive
evidenced eligible deferrals, flag it for owner review with the blocker and a concrete proposed
next step. Count actual opportunities for the work to proceed; unavailable
permissions or unresolved prerequisites must remain visible. The flag does not
authorise bypassing protected duties, review requirements or resource limits.

## Research and knowledge transfer

Each iteration must distinguish mathematical correctness, novelty, measured
knowledge transfer and editorial acceptance. A useful failed attempt belongs
in the record. New questions require literature checks and a stated reason to
study them. Verifier changes and claimed novel results require independent
review. No automated process may declare a journal submission accepted.

An iteration should preserve its evidence even when it fails, update the
applicable procedural lesson where justified, and prepare a teaching companion
when there is something useful to explain. Permitted library curation retains
the exact source version, attribution and licence. Publicly licensed material
remains available on its stated terms. Access to additional private library
material is considered through individual enquiries, case by case; the research
worker cannot grant access or promise membership terms.

## Evidence-led communication

When there is a useful public result, the worker prepares a promotion packet:
the public resource, exact version, verified outcome, limitations, audience,
teaching value and a concise description. It records whether the packet was
handed off and whether publication was subsequently verified.

The existing **GitHub Adoption**, **Daily Website Resource Refresh** and
**Website Care and Reach** lanes handle their established responsibilities.
The research worker supplies evidence to those lanes and respects their
deduplication and single-writer arrangements. Website publication remains with
the designated publisher; the mathematics worker does not create a parallel
website publishing process. Promotion must describe the available evidence and
actual publication status, including an unsuccessful or limited result where
that is educationally useful.

Include [Albert Tan's website](https://www.alberttansinhon.com/), the GitHub
profile and relevant journal learning links in the affected-destination check.
Record each destination as current, queued, blocked or not applicable, with its
source version, evidence and next owner. Propagate material corrections to
earlier summaries. A handoff remains open until its receiving stage is verified.

Research authority does not authorise bulk email, unsolicited outreach or
journal self-acceptance. The journal's ordinary independent review and human
decision process remains applicable.

## Resources and access

The configured task uses existing access and introduces no paid model API or
new service. It must report unavailable execution or connector access rather
than invent a result. Pricing and access administration are separate, deferred
work. They do not delay research, evidence preservation or permitted teaching.
The worker cannot activate billing or grant private-library access. Private
enquiry records and confidential editorial material must never enter public
research records.
